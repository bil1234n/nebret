1. Who we are

Nebret runs an online marketplace in Ethiopia where verified partner companies (real estate developers, car dealers, e-bike and electronics sellers) list cars, houses, apartments, land, commercial units, e-bikes and electronics. 5. Equb

  • An Equb is a savings group. Every member pays the same contribution every round, and every member receives the full pot exactly once. The draw only decides the order in which members are paid. Equb is not gambling and not a loan: nobody receives another member's money and nobody pays interest.
  • Draws are random and provably fair – you can verify any draw in the app – unless a group is set to manual selection, which is shown in the group.
  • To stay eligible for a draw, a member must be up to date with contributions. After a few paid rounds we ask for an Equb member profile (photo, ID and monthly income) to confirm identity.
  • A member who joins after the first round pays the rounds that were already drawn. No late penalty is charged.
  • Members who fall far behind may be warned, held out of draws, and finally removed from the group under the group's rules. What a removed member paid and owes is settled as described in the group's terms.
  • Each Equb group has its own terms (amount, frequency, duration, payout and fees), which you accept when you join.
