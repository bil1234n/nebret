This policy explains what personal data the Nebret app and website collect, why, who we share it with and how you can delete it. Nebret (“we”) runs a marketplace for cars, houses, apartments, land, e-bikes and electronics sold by verified partner companies, property Equb savings groups, installment plans and community crowdfunding in Ethiopia. Questions: {{ support_email }}.

Data we collect

  • Account: full name, Ethiopian mobile number, email address (optional), password (stored only as a secure hash), preferred language and your referral code.
  • Buyer details: the name on the sale contract or title, national ID / Kebele ID number, date of birth, city and delivery address you enter when you order.
  • Orders and payments: items you order, reservations, down payments and installments, Equb places and contributions, crowdfunding gifts, refunds and payout vouchers. Payments are made on telebirr or Chapa – we receive the amount, status and transaction reference, never your PIN, card or bank login.
  • Questions to sellers: messages and viewing or test-drive requests you send about a listing, and the items you save.
  • Referrals and giveaways: who joined with your code and the free giveaway entries you earned.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can send order, installment and Equb reminders.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files, and the app contains no advertising or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To place your orders, hold the unit you chose, schedule installments and arrange the handover with the seller.
  • To run Equb groups: record contributions, run draws, pay out winners and send reminders.
  • To process payments and refunds, prevent fraud and keep the financial records we are required to keep.
  • To answer your questions and send service messages. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • The partner company selling the item – your name, phone, buyer details and payment status for that order, so they can prepare the contract and hand the property over to you.
  • Payment providers (telebirr, Chapa) – the amount and reference needed to take your payment.
  • Service providers that host our servers and database and deliver push notifications, only to run the service.
  • Authorities when the law requires it (for example for title or vehicle registration).

Other Equb members see you only by place number, not by name or phone.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When your account is deleted we erase your name, phone number, email, ID and address details, saved items and notifications. Payment, order and Equb records are kept without your name for as long as financial and tax law requires.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage. Handover passes are signed so they cannot be forged.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details in the app (Profile) or by contacting us.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on this web page. Your account is suspended at once and deleted after our team's review.
  • Turn off notifications at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

Nebret is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
